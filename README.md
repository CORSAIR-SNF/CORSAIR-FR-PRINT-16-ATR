# CORSAIR-FR-PRINT-16 – ATR data for 16th c. French prints

![characters badge](badges/characters.svg) ![regions badge](badges/regions.svg) ![lines badge](badges/lines.svg) ![files badge](badges/files.svg)


## About

This repository contains groundtruth for ATR dedicated to 16th c. French prints. Data is distributed in ALTO-XML.

## Contents

This repository contains:
| Author | Year | Title (short) |  NB of Transcripted Pages | Source |
|--------|------|---------------|-------|--------|
| Louis Régnier de La Planche  | 1576  | *Histoire de l'estat de France* | 25  | [BnF catalogue](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb31189656s) |
| Simon Goulart  | 1578 | *Mémoires de l'Estat de France sous Charles Neufiesme*  | 36 | [e-rara](https://doi.org/10.3931/e-rara-3394) |
| Lancelot Voisin de La Popelinière  | 1572 | *La vraye et entiere histoire des troubles et choses memorables*  | 18  | [Internet Archive](https://n2t.net/ark:/13960/s2f2wsg8ch9) |
| Lancelot Voisin de La Popelinière | 1581  | *L'histoire de France (Advertissemens necessaires)* | 10  | [Gallica](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/bpt6k8717136q) |
| Agrippa d'Aubigné | 1616 | *L'histoire universelle*  | 23 | [e-rara](https://doi.org/10.3931/e-rara-57143) |
